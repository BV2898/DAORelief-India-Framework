DAORelief India Framework

An automated on-chain Proof of Concept (PoC) custom-tailored to circumvent administrative roadblocks and coordinate rapid financial distribution across the Indian subcontinent. By linking decentralized multi-sig smart vaults with automated data validation systems, critical capital shifts transparently from global donors straight to verified local field operations during major national crises.

🔥 Western Ghats Forest Fires Trigged by thermal anomaly indicators and hot-spot infrared satellite streams.
🌧 Monsoon Heavy Rainfall Automated activations tied directly to IMD micro-gauge metrics exceeding cloudburst levels.
🌊 Coastal Cyclones & Tsunamis Immediate capital processing mapped against bay-sensor tsunami wave alerts or wind telemetry.
🏢 Urban Structural Collapses Emergency response funding triggered via local fire service and seismic network logs.

Structural Innovation Vectors (India Focus)

  • Leakage Proof Operations: Eliminates intermediary bureaucratic tiers, ensuring funds move explicitly to certified accounts.
  • Immediate Liquidity Routing: Skips traditional weeks-long processing delays to secure financial execution in early critical windows.
  • Public Compliance Trails: Leverages public cryptographic ledger entries to make post-disaster accountability tracking effortless.

Technical Realities & Implementation Roadblocks

  • Data Dependency Errors: Complete structural dependency on local weather APIs, radar infrastructure, or third-party sensor hardware networks.
  • Field Verification Limits: Direct integration with physical identities requires highly secure off-chain coordination layers.
  • Network Gas Price Surges: Spikes in transaction fee overheads on public chains can restrict micro-payments during periods of high congestion.
